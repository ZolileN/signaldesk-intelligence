
SIGNALDESK AFRICA
Canonical Intelligence Data Model and Event/Situation Ontology
Version: 1.0Status: Foundational Technical SpecificationPurpose: Define the canonical objects, relationships, classifications and lifecycle states used by SignalDesk Africa.

1. Design Principle
Every source in SignalDesk must eventually produce a common intelligence representation.
SOURCE
   ↓
OBSERVATION
   ↓
CLAIM
   ↓
ENTITY
   ↓
EVENT
   ↓
SITUATION
   ↓
TRAJECTORY
   ↓
EXPOSURE
   ↓
ACTION
VectaNews, radio sensors, social monitoring and future data sources must not create isolated data models.
They must all feed the same canonical intelligence layer.

4. Observation
An observation is the raw information captured from a source.
Examples:
A news article.
A radio segment.
A social media post.
A government document.
A company statement.
Observation
├── id
├── source_id
├── content_type

├── title
├── url
├── published_at
├── captured_at
├── language
├── location
├── media_reference
├── transcript
├── hash
└── provenance
The original observation must be preserved.
The system must never destroy the original evidence when generating derived intelligence.

7. Entity Resolution
The same real-world entity may appear under multiple names.
"Cyril Ramaphosa"
"President Ramaphosa"
"Ramaphosa"
"CR"
The system should resolve them to:
CANONICAL ENTITY:
Cyril Matamela Ramaphosa
The entity resolution pipeline:
RAW MENTION
     ↓
NORMALISATION
     ↓
ALIAS MATCHING
     ↓
CONTEXT ANALYSIS
     ↓
ENTITY RESOLUTION
     ↓
CANONICAL ENTITY
The system must support:
Aliases.
Acronyms.
Spelling variants.
Transliteration.
Multilingual names.
Titles.
Informal names.

11. Situation
A situation is a collection of related events forming a meaningful developing condition.
An event is:
Something happened.
A situation is:
A condition is developing.
Example:
Community Complaint
       +
Public Meeting
       +
Protest Announcement
       +
Road Blockade
       +
Political Intervention
       ↓
COMMUNITY-INDUSTRY CONFLICT SITUATION

Communications Department:
Prepare public messaging.

22. Canonical Relationship Model
The central relationship graph is:
SOURCE
   ↓ produces
OBSERVATION
   ↓ contains
CLAIM
   ↓ references
ENTITY
   ↓ participates in
EVENT
   ↓ connected to
EVENT
   ↓ forms
SITUATION
   ↓ affects
ENTITY
   ↓ owned/operated by
CUSTOMER
   ↓ creates
EXPOSURE
   ↓ creates
IMPACT
   ↓ produces
RECOMMENDATION
This is the canonical intelligence model for SignalDesk Africa.
