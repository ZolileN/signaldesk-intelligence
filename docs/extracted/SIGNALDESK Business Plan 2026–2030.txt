Executive Summary
SignalDesk is an Africa-first situational intelligence platform designed to help organisations understand what is changing in the world, how situations are developing, how they are exposed, and what they should do next.
The platform ingests information from:
News.
Radio.
Podcasts.
Government sources.
Public documents.
Social and digital sources.
Market and regulatory information.
Customer-provided internal context.
It transforms this fragmented information into continuously evolving intelligence objects called Situations.
Each Situation answers eight questions:
What is happening?
Is it getting worse or better?
Where is it happening?
Who is involved?
What is driving it?
What happens next?
How am I exposed?
What should I do?
The product family consists of:
Vecta News
The discovery and news intelligence layer.
Qisimah Audio Insights
The audio intelligence layer, particularly valuable for radio, interviews, local-language broadcasts and public discussions.
SignalDesk
The core intelligence and decision layer.
The strategic opportunity is to build a platform that sits between:
Raw information

        ↓
Risk intelligence
        ↓

Existing companies are strong in individual parts of this value chain.
Media-monitoring companies aggregate coverage.

SignalDesk's opportunity is to build an Africa-first platform that combines:

1. The Strategic Thesis
Africa is entering a period of increasing:
Infrastructure investment.
Energy investment.
Mining and critical-mineral development.
Urbanisation.
Political competition.
Regulatory change.
Geopolitical competition.
Climate and environmental pressure.
Digital transformation.
PwC's 2025–2050 infrastructure outlook projects combined annual infrastructure expenditure in South Africa, Nigeria, Ghana and Kenya to rise from approximately US$54 billion to US$96 billion by 2050, a 77% increase. The report also highlights the convergence of power, transport and digital infrastructure, and the importance of early community engagement.
At the same time, African venture activity remained comparatively resilient in 2025. AVCA reports 506 venture deals worth US$3.9 billion, with climate-related ventures accounting for US$1.5 billion, or 40% of deal value.
The more capital enters complex environments, the more valuable it becomes to understand:
What is changing around an investment, asset, project or organisation before the change becomes an operational crisis.
This is the market opportunity for SignalDesk.

AlphaSense
AlphaSense is a major competitor for investors, corporate strategy and research teams.

AlphaSense is increasingly moving from search and research toward always-on AI workflows and agentic research execution.
