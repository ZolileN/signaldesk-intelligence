The Core Data Flow
The entire process can be simplified to:
RADIO STREAM
     ↓
AUDIO
     ↓
SEGMENT
     ↓
CLASSIFY
     ↓
SPEECH
     ↓
TRANSCRIBE
     ↓
EXTRACT
     ↓
EVENT
     ↓
SITUATION
     ↓
TRAJECTORY
     ↓
EXPOSURE
     ↓
ACTION

This is the fundamental transformation:
Unstructured audio → structured intelligence → decision support

The Three Listening Modes
The most important architectural feature is that SignalDesk should not have only one listening mode.
1. Continuous Awareness
The system remains connected to the stream.
Purpose:
Maintain stream health.
Detect unexpected speech.
Detect breaking news.
Monitor unusual broadcasts.
Identify changes in programming.
Stream
  ↓
Low-cost audio monitoring
  ↓
Unexpected signal?

If something unusual occurs, the system can activate deeper processing.

2. Scheduled Intelligence Listening
This is based on the station's News Clock.
Example:
11:57 → Begin high-priority capture
12:00 → Expected news bulletin
12:00–12:15 → Full intelligence processing
12:15 → Extract stories and events

The same applies to:
10:00
10:30
11:00
11:30
12:00
12:30

depending on the station.
The important thing is that SignalDesk knows:
“This is when important information is likely to be broadcast.”

3. Adaptive Event-Triggered Listening
This is the most powerful mode.
Suppose a 10:00 news bulletin says:
A community has threatened to shut down a major road tomorrow.
SignalDesk detects:
Potential Situation:
Community X → Road Shutdown Threat

The system then increases monitoring:
Community X
Road
Municipality
Political actors
Organisers
Related companies

The next time these entities are mentioned on radio, SignalDesk gives the audio higher priority.
Normal Station Monitoring
          ↓
Potential Event Detected
          ↓
Entity Watchlist Created
          ↓
Adaptive Monitoring
          ↓
More Evidence
          ↓
Situation Confidence Increases

This is where the system moves beyond passive monitoring.

For every station:

Initially configured

This creates an intelligent, self-correcting scheduler.

The Key Design Principle
The system should not ask:
“Should we transcribe this audio?”
It should ask:
“Does this audio have the potential to change our understanding of a situation?”
That means processing priority can change dynamically.
For example:
Music:
Priority 0

Advertisement:
Priority 0

Routine sports:
Priority 1

Normal talk:
Priority 2

News:
Priority 5

Political interview:
Priority 7

Community grievance:
Priority 8

Breaking news:
Priority 10

Known situation escalation:
Priority 10

The priority engine can determine:
Capture
↓
Transcribe
↓
Translate
↓
Analyse
↓
Alert

or:
Capture
↓
Discard


The Final Concept
I would describe the SignalDesk radio architecture as:
A Programming-Aware, Event-Driven Broadcast Intelligence Sensor
It does four things:
1. It knows where to listen
Station Registry

2. It knows when to listen deeply
News Clock + Programme Schedule

3. It knows what deserves processing
Audio Classification + Intelligence Priority

4. It knows what the information means
Transcription → Event → Situation → Trajectory → Exposure → Action

The fundamental architecture is therefore:
Radio is the sensor. The broadcast is the raw signal. The transcript is the observation. The event is the structured data. The situation is the intelligence. The recommendation is the product.
This fits perfectly with SignalDesk's broader core promise:
What is happening?Is it getting worse or better?Where is it happening?Who is involved?What is driving it?What happens next?How am I exposed?What should I do?
